Supply-side Risks
Originate from suppliers — visible at Tier 1, hidden below. The most common category and the one most companies think they manage. They usually don't — not below Tier 1.
6 risk types
📈
Demand-side Risks
Originate from customers and markets. Less discussed but equally dangerous. A demand collapse destroys a business as effectively as a supply disruption.
3 risk types
🌍
Environmental Risks
Originate outside the supply chain in the physical and geopolitical world. Hardest to detect. Many are black swans — requiring resilience planning, not risk scoring.
3 risk types
⚙️
Infrastructure Risks
Affect the systems supply chains run on — logistics networks, digital systems, regulatory frameworks. Often hidden. Require different monitoring and detection methods.
3 risk types
⚡
Foundational Emerging Risks
Identified by Professor Yossi Sheffi at MIT in 2024–2025. Most organisations have not yet incorporated either into their formal risk systems. AGNA addresses them because forward-looking risk management must account for tomorrow's risks.
