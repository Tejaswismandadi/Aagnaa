What the dashboard makes possible
A risk manager opens this dashboard at 7am. The Stuttgart Seating Z-score has moved overnight. The U33 TTS has crossed the amber threshold. The CBAM deadline is 90 days away. Three specific actions are required today - not a quarterly report saying "financial distress remains elevated." Every metric drives a decision.
