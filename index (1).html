Why AGNA exists

Most companies don't have a risk management problem.
They have a visibility problem.

Research shows 70%+ of supply disruptions show early warning signals weeks before impact. The gap is not luck - it is visibility, methodology, and the discipline to act before the crisis hits.

👁️
See what others cannot
Diamond dependencies, geographic concentration, sub-tier opacity - the risks that cause the most damage are almost always the ones nobody was watching for. AGNA makes them visible before they strike.
⚖️
Score with precision
Not "high / medium / low." Probability × Impact × Velocity = a Risk Priority Number grounded in data. Every score is traceable to program revenue, supplier inventory, and engineering lead times.
🛡️
Survive what cannot be predicted
COVID. Suez Canal. Taiwan semiconductor crisis. Black swans cannot be scored - but organisations can be built to survive them. The survival window concept shows exactly how.
