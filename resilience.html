Before the playbooks

Two concepts that change how you think about black swans

Viability vs resilience
Resilience is the ability to recover to the previous state. Viability is the ability to adapt when recovery to the previous state is not possible. Some disruptions you recover from. Others - a permanent geopolitical shift, a decade-long EV transition - require the supply chain itself to permanently adapt. Confusing the two is how companies spend years trying to recover to a world that no longer exists.
The white space concept
Most companies design their supply chains for normal variation - ±20% demand, standard lead time variance, typical logistics disruptions. Black swans live in the white space beyond that design envelope. The companies that survive COVID, Suez, and Taiwan are not necessarily smarter - they built buffer into the white space as a deliberate investment in resilience capability. AGNA's survival window extension is exactly this.
